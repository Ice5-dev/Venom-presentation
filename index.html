01 Venom (VENOM) cryptocurrency

Venom: Fast, Scalable—and Risky?

Presented by: Isaac Mccorry
Cryptocurrency · November 5, 2026

02 Context

Who, Where, and When?

  • Built by the Venom Foundation, incorporated in 2022
  • First foundation licensed by ADGM (Abu Dhabi) to run a blockchain and issue utility tokens
  • Born in a bear market, as the UAE became a regulated crypto hub
  • Founders: Abutalibov, Kheriba, Knez, Khan, Garcia Isaaev
  • Mainnet launched March 2024

03 Complication

What Was Missing?

  • Older blockchains could be slow and expensive
  • Institutions needed regulated, scalable infrastructure
  • Stablecoins, CBDCs and tokenized real-world assets needed a fast network
  • Traditional finance and Web3 were still disconnected

04 Turning point

What Venom Built

  • Layer 0 + Layer 1 architecture
  • Dynamic sharding + Threaded Virtual Machine
  • Proof-of-stake + specialized workchains
  • Up to 150,000 TPS, sub-second finality
  • $VENOM: fees, staking, DeFi and GameFi

05 Resolution

Did It Succeed?

  • Technology: Yes — dynamic sharding is live
  • Adoption: struggling against established Layer-1s
  • Delisted from OKX and CoinW
  • Trades at $0.009–$0.015, about 97% below its peak

06 Closing

Buy, Sell, or Hold?

  • Quiz answer: Dynamic sharding
  • Decision: Sell — at best a high-risk hold
  • Why: only ~25% of supply circulating, token-unlock dilution, thin liquidity, money rotating to Bitcoin
  • Plus: advanced, regulated technology; only short-term traders see upside

Strong technology does not guarantee market success.
